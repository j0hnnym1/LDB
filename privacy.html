Privacy & cookie notice

Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short

  • No cookies. This website stores nothing on your device.
  • No tracking. No analytics, advertising or social-media tools.
  • No third parties. Fonts, images and scripts all come from this website.

That is why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

1. Who is responsible

letsdobusiness is the controller of the personal data described in this notice. For any privacy question or request, email info@letsdobusiness.gr.

2. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not store information on your device or read information from it. In particular, it uses:

  • no cookies, first-party or third-party;
  • no local storage, session storage or other browser storage;
  • no tracking pixels, fingerprinting, analytics or advertising tools;
  • no embedded social-media buttons, maps or videos.

Because nothing is stored or read, no consent is required under Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC), as implemented in Greece by Law 3471/2006. If this ever changes, we will update this notice and ask for your consent before anything is stored.

3. Fonts, images and scripts

Everything this website needs to display is served from this website itself. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts, content delivery networks or any other third party when you visit. A Content Security Policy enforces this: browsers block any request to another domain.

4. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (United States). To deliver a page, your browser necessarily sends your IP address and standard request details, such as the page requested, the date and time, and your browser type, to the host. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

We do not receive these logs and do not use them to identify you or build profiles. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub may process this data in the United States and states that it complies with the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For details, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

5. When you email us

If you write to us, we process your email address, your name and anything you include in your message. We use this only to answer you and, where relevant, to prepare or carry out a collaboration with you.

The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose, or longer where the law requires it, for example for tax and accounting records. We never sell your data or use it for marketing.

6. Links to other websites

Links to other websites, such as nngroup.gr, take you to services with their own privacy practices. This notice does not cover them.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, and to have it corrected or erased. You may also restrict or object to its processing and ask to receive it in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, email info@letsdobusiness.gr; we will reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr) or with the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live or work.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

8. Changes to this notice

We will update this page whenever the website changes in a way that affects your data, for example if we ever add a contact form. The date at the top shows the latest version.
