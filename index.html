About us

letsdobusiness is a strategic management consultancy founded in Ptolemaida, Greece, in 2004. We act as investment and co-funding experts, delivering technical assistance to SMEs across sectors — wine and agri-food, services, commerce, IT, and energy production.

We bring proven experience in project design, management, and evaluation, EU-funded international projects, and multicultural teamwork — and we stay involved until the results are there, not just the recommendations.
